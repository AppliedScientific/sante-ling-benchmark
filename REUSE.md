# Reuse and release status

This is a private release candidate for inspection. The company and study owner have not approved a public visibility change or a general license for the data and scripts. No blanket open-source or open-data license is assigned by this package.

Before a public release, record the owners' approval, exact public wording and the chosen code/data license. Confirm the right to distribute model outputs, reviewer annotations and quoted material. The source register links to publishers and regulators; it does not grant rights over their works. Full articles and the retrieval corpus are excluded.

The files support independent reanalysis. They do not certify the judgments, establish clinical safety or imply endorsement by the model providers. The first AI judge identity/version and the returned reviewer's identity remain unknown in the supplied records.

Only an explicit owner decision should change repository visibility. Keeping the candidate private does not prevent the recipient team from inspecting the complete supplied data and running the offline checks.
