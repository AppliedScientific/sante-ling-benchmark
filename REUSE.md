# Reuse and release status

The repository owner authorized public access on 13 November 2026. This release contains the direct-model dataset and offline analysis only. No blanket open-source or open-data license is assigned by this package. The visibility change does not add a license or establish provider endorsement.

The source register links to publishers and regulators; it does not grant rights over their works. Full articles and the retrieval corpus are excluded. Model outputs, reviewer annotations and quoted material retain their applicable terms.

The files support independent reanalysis. They do not certify the judgments or establish clinical safety. The first AI judge identity/version and the returned reviewer's identity remain unknown in the supplied records. Original answers and first grades are unchanged; returned labels remain a separate analysis view.
